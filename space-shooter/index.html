One year of the contribution graph, turned into a sky you can clear. Every block is a real contribution day — its colour is that day’s heatmap level, its health is that day’s commit count, and the thirteen waves follow the calendar in order.

Use the arrow keys or W A S D to fly, space to fire commits, P to pause and Enter to start.

←→↑↓ or WASD fly space fire P pause touch: drag to fly, it fires on its own

Waves, block health and colours come from data/contributions.json — the same file that draws the heatmap. No third-party services, no trackers, no build.
